not notarized yet

this alpha is open-source, but not notarized by Apple yet. first launch may be blocked.

  1. download the `.dmg` and move Downshift into Applications.
  2. in Applications, right-click Downshift and choose Open.
  3. confirm Open in the Gatekeeper prompt.
  4. if needed: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
